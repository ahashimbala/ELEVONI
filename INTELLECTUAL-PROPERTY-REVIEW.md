# Elevoni intellectual-property ownership checklist

The source archive does not prove legal ownership of every line of code, brand asset, photo, font, icon, voice, model or other material. Complete and retain the following records before commercial distribution.

## Company and brand
- Confirm the CAC company name and registered details against the current CAC certificate.
- Confirm whether the Elevoni / Elevoni Farms names, logos, domain names and social accounts are owned by the company or licensed to it.
- Record any trademark applications, registrations or third-party objections. Do not use the ® symbol unless a mark is registered.

## Software and contributions
- List every developer, designer, contractor, intern and collaborator who contributed code, UI, copy, data, documentation or designs.
- Obtain written IP assignment or a sufficiently broad commercial licence from each contributor, where required.
- Confirm that student, internship, employer, university, competition or grant terms do not claim rights in contributed work.
- Confirm ownership/licence of GitHub repositories, domains, deployment projects and cloud accounts.
- Keep dated source history and signed contribution agreements.

## Third-party content and services
- Inventory photographs, videos, illustrations, generated assets, fonts, icons, maps, sound and voice assets.
- Retain invoices, licences, releases, source links and attribution notices.
- Confirm that AI-generated or AI-assisted content complies with the relevant provider terms and does not reproduce protected third-party assets.
- Review every npm dependency and Android/Capacitor SDK for licence and attribution obligations.
- Review the Dynamic ASR model weights separately from inference code. Do not assume an open-source code licence grants unrestricted commercial use of model weights or training data.
- Confirm that vendor-uploaded content is licensed to the vendor and that vendor terms give Elevoni the rights needed to host, review and display it.

## Business documents to retain
- CAC incorporation documents.
- Contributor and contractor agreements.
- IP assignment/licence agreements.
- Brand and domain ownership records.
- Third-party asset licences and attribution notices.
- Dependency licence report.
- Model and AI-provider licence terms.
- Vendor terms and content permissions.

This is a due-diligence checklist, not a legal conclusion that Elevoni owns every asset in the repository.
